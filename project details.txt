# Luxury Real Estate Platform – Complete Product & Design Specification

## Project Vision

Build a modern, premium real-estate platform that combines elegant luxury aesthetics, subtle cinematic storytelling, and lightweight 3D experiences.

The website should feel sophisticated, trustworthy, and high-end without looking overloaded with animations or heavy visual effects.

The goal is to create a platform that looks suitable for a luxury real-estate developer, property consultancy, or premium residential project.

The experience should prioritize usability, performance, responsiveness, and business functionality over excessive visual effects.

Design philosophy:

* 70% Modern Luxury UI
* 20% Cinematic Motion
* 10% Lightweight 3D Experience

The website should feel premium, modern, professional, and visually impressive while maintaining excellent performance.
